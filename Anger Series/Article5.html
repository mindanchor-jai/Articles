Hook

Your org chart tells you who reports to whom. Your values document tells you what the culture believes. Your strategy deck tells you where the company is going.

None of them tell you the truth.

The truth is this: most organizations unconsciously optimize around the emotional limitations of their most powerful person. The formal strategy exists on paper. The actual strategy is: keep the leader comfortable.

This is the Shadow Governance Architecture. And it is running your company right now.
