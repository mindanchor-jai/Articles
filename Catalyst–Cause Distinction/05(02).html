Triggers Release, They Do Not Create

The last reading named a gap: reactions that outsize their triggers. This one answers where the extra size comes from. It was never manufactured in the moment — it was stored beforehand, and the trigger only opened the valve.

Where does the energy for a disproportionate reaction actually come from?

Fig. 2 — the reservoir, and the drop that finally overflowed it.

the glass and the last dropan old, useful image

Picture a glass filled slowly, one drop at a time, over weeks. Then one more drop — no different from the hundred before it — and the glass overflows.

No one would blame that last drop for the whole glass of water. Yet this is exactly how a minor trigger gets blamed for an entire reaction.

The trigger is real. It happened right before the reaction, so it looks like the cause. But it was never the source of what came out.

reservoir, not source

The Trigger Explains the Timing, Not the Size

Accumulated, unexpressed strain behaves like stored potential energy. It sits inside a system — a person, a relationship, a team — until something releases it. The trigger is the release valve. It was never the reservoir.

The common assumption

This reaction was caused by what just happened.

What's actually true

This reaction was caused by what was already stored. What just happened only released it.

why the same person keeps explodingover different things, every time

How This Shows Up

The mechanism behind a disproportionate reaction has a recognizable shape of its own.

  • A container overflows from one final drop, no larger than any of the ones before it.
  • Sudden exhaustion right after an outburst — the feeling of having "let something out."
  • Realizing, mid-argument, that the argument was never really about the thing being argued.
  • The same person, the same kind of explosion, over completely different topics each time.

This is the mechanism itself — the piece of the Catalyst–Cause Distinction that Disproportionate Volatility exists to demonstrate. Once the source of the energy is visible, one question remains: what happens when the trigger is removed? That's the final reading.
